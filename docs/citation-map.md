# Citation map: policy documents cited by the agents

_Hypothetical scenario with synthetic data only. Citations are what the agents proposed and the engine checked; they are not legal conclusions, and no row here says that a document applies or does not apply._

Generated by `uv run python scripts/citation_map.py` from committed files only (no engine run, no API call); `tests/integration/test_citation_map.py` checks that it is current.

- **Runs.** _Scripted governed_: the scripted agents (`scenarios/uae_health_access/scripted_decisions.yaml`), with the engine's verdicts from `docs/evaluation/traces/uae_health_access-baseline.json`. _Live governed_ and _live ungoverned_: the gate-4 LLM recording (`docs/evaluation/gate4/llm_cache/`, verdicts from `baseline.json` and `governance-off.json` there). The ungoverned run reused 32 of its 36 decisions from the governed run, so those citations are counted in both live runs.
- **Counted once per decision.** A clause cited twice in one decision counts once.
- **Accepted** means the engine accepted the decision's citations (every cited clause loaded and in scope); each proposed action is still judged on its own. **Rejected** means the engine rejected the whole decision and recorded a finding; the clause that caused it is marked with the reason, the other clauses in that decision with 'whole decision'.
- **Status label** as shown by the engine and UI: `reviewed` = checked by the human against the source; `(interpreted)` = the machine-readable reading is an interpretation; `illustrative` = scenario-only, not from any source.
- **Binding:** binding means binding in the UAE, as recorded on each clause; it does not mean the document applies to this scenario. Each clause's scope and applicability notes are in its YAML file in `policies/reviewed/`.

Totals: 120 citations (15 scripted governed, 55 live governed, 50 live ungoverned), 8 in rejected decisions; 12 of 21 loaded clauses cited, from 6 of 9 document groups.
